Price and what it covers
€1,690 to €1,860 per person, all-in, varying by room. Currency EUR.

Included: your bed for the 7 nights; the private chef, 6 breakfasts and 5 dinners; the group
shuttle GVA to the chalet and back; taxes, incidentals and tips.

Not included: flights, ski pass, equipment rental, and nights out.
