The week, day by day
Eight days. "Chef" means the private chef cooks that meal at the chalet; "own" means you are on
your own; "none" means no chalet meal that day. Open days have no plan fixed yet.

Sat 30 Jan — Arrival, shuttle from GVA, settle in. Breakfast none, dinner chef.
Sun 31 Jan — Chill drinks at Le Rond Point des Pistes, Méribel. Breakfast chef, dinner chef.
Mon 1 Feb — La Folie Douce, Méribel–Courchevel. Breakfast chef, dinner chef.
Tue 2 Feb — Open, plans to be decided. Breakfast chef, dinner chef.
Wed 3 Feb — Le Cap Horn, Courchevel 1850. Breakfast chef, dinner chef.
Thu 4 Feb — Final night out. Breakfast chef, dinner on your own.
Fri 5 Feb — Open, last ski day. Breakfast chef, dinner on your own.
Sat 6 Feb — Departure, chalet pickup 04:00. Breakfast none, dinner none.

The open days are Tue 2 Feb and Fri 5 Feb.
