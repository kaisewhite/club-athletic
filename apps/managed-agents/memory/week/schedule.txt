The week, day by day
"Chef" means the private chef cooks that meal at the chalet; "own" means you are on your own;
"none" means no chalet meal that day.

Fri 29 Jan — Arrive in Geneva (optional): land, use the hotel shuttle options, and spend the day
in Geneva as a buffer before the Saturday transfer. Breakfast and dinner own.
Sat 30 Jan — Arrive at GVA, transfer to the chalet in Méribel, settle in, and optionally watch NFL
playoff games. Breakfast none, dinner chef.
Sun 31 Jan — Skiing and après drinks at Rond Point; Club Athletic buys rounds. Breakfast chef,
dinner chef.
Mon 1 Feb — Ski and go to La Folie Douce from 3–5 p.m. Breakfast chef, dinner chef.
Tue 2 Feb — Ski day, then après at randomly assigned chalets followed by a night out. Breakfast
chef, dinner chef.
Wed 3 Feb — Courchevel day: Cap Horn, then going out in Courchevel; no buses. Breakfast chef,
dinner none.
Thu 4 Feb — Final big night out at Doron Pub. Breakfast chef, dinner none.
Fri 5 Feb — Chill ski day at Rond Point; many people leave in the morning. Breakfast chef, dinner
chef.
Sat 6 Feb — Early-morning transfer to Geneva and flights home; chalet pickup 04:00. Breakfast none,
dinner none.
