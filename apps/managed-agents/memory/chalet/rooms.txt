Bedroom layout and bed types
This is the fixed layout of the chalet. It is NOT who is staying where, and it is NOT what is
still free. For occupancy or availability call getRoomsByFloor and getOpenSpots every time.

Upper floor, code R11 / F21
  Bedroom 1 — master double, 2 spots

Middle floor, code R10 / F21
  Bedroom 2 — double, 2 spots
  Bedroom 3 — twin, 2 spots
  Bedroom 4 — quad bunk, 4 spots
  Bedroom 5 — bunk cabin, 2 spots

Lower floor, code R9 / F12
  Bedroom 6 — double, 2 spots
  Bedroom 7 — double, 2 spots
  Bedroom 8 — quad bunk, 4 spots

Eight bedrooms, 20 spots in total, which is why the chalet sleeps up to 20.
Most rooms are en-suite with a balcony or terrace. A couple of the bunk rooms share a shower
room and may not have balcony access. Which specific room is en-suite or has a balcony is not
recorded, so do not claim it for an individual room.
