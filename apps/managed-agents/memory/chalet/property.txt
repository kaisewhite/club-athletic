The chalet: Falcon Lodge F
Address: 269 Rte de l'Altiport, 73550 Les Allues, France. Central Méribel, about 200 m from the slopes.
Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=269+Rte+de+l'Altiport,+73550+Les+Allues,+France
Size: 326 m² over three levels, 8 bedrooms, sleeps 10 to 20.
It is two apartments combined: F12 (sleeps 4 to 8) and F21 (sleeps 6 to 12).
In the chalet: multiple lounges and dining areas with two fireplaces; a main kitchen plus a
lower-level kitchenette; a private outdoor hot tub, two saunas and a fitness room; ski lockers
with boot warmers; underground parking and private laundry.
Shared in the residence: indoor pool, hammam, sauna and massage rooms; an on-site ski shop and
equipment rental.
Source section for this material: Chalet.
