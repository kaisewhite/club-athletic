Flights.

Everything is timed around the group shuttle at Geneva (GVA).

Getting there · Saturday 30 Jan
08:30
Your flight needs to land in Geneva by 8:30 AM

Aim for 8:00 or earlier for bags and delays. Shuttle leaves GVA 10:30–11:00.

Land later and you'll likely miss it — plan your own transfer.

Going home · Saturday 6 Feb
11:00
Book a flight leaving Geneva at 11:00 AM or later

Chalet pickup 4:15–4:30 AM, ~2 h to GVA, then check-in and security.

Anything earlier and you won't make it through security in time.

Option · Arrive Friday
Overnight near GVA

Fly Thursday evening, land Friday morning (e.g. EWR → GVA 5:35 PM → 7:25 AM), sleep by the airport, meet the shuttle Saturday.

Geneva Marriott — free shuttle, under 5 min
Hilton Geneva — free shuttle every ~20 min, 4:20 AM–11:40 PM

Shuttle.

One 49-seat bus each way for people, luggage and skis. About 2 hours door to door.

To the chalet · Saturday 30 Jan
10:30

The bus leaves Geneva Airport between 10:30 and 11:00 AM. Have your bags with you at the meeting point.

Back to the airport · Saturday 6 Feb
04:15

The bus picks us up at the chalet between 4:15 and 4:30 AM. Pack the night before.

Meeting point and driver contact will be posted here closer to the date.
